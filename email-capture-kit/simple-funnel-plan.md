# Simple Funnel Plan

## Goal
Turn website visitors into email subscribers using the free Spring Backyard Wildlife Checklist.

## Basic flow
1. Visitor lands on the site
2. Visitor sees checklist offer
3. Visitor enters email
4. Visitor gets welcome email with checklist link
5. Visitor returns to the blog/site over time

## Best places for the signup form
- homepage checklist section
- dedicated signup page
- bottom of blog posts
- optional small callout later in the site footer or sidebar

## What matters most right now
- clear free offer
- simple form
- welcome email that delivers the checklist
- consistent traffic from Pinterest and content

## Not necessary yet
- complicated funnels
- multiple lead magnets
- automation overload
- advanced segmentation
