## 2) 7 Easy Ways to Make Your Yard More Bird-Friendly

### Goal
Focus on birds specifically, since they are one of the most relatable entry points.

### Outline
- Intro: You do not need a huge property to attract more birds
- 1: Add a bird bath or shallow water source
- 2: Keep feeders clean and placed well
- 3: Offer shelter with shrubs, brush, or evergreens
- 4: Plant things that provide seeds, berries, or cover
- 5: Reduce disturbance around nesting season
- 6: Avoid over-cleaning every natural area
- 7: Observe what species already visit and build from there
- Wrap-up: Build a better bird yard one improvement at a time
