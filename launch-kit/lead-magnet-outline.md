# Spring Backyard Wildlife Checklist

## Goal
Give beginners a quick, encouraging way to improve their yard for birds, butterflies, pollinators, and small wildlife.

## Working Title
Spring Backyard Wildlife Checklist

## Subtitle
Simple steps to help your yard feel more alive this season.

## Suggested Structure

### Page 1 — Quick intro
- Short welcome
- Explain that small changes matter
- Encourage people to start with what they can do this week

### Page 2 — Checklist
- Clean and refill bird feeders
- Refresh hummingbird feeders when the season starts
- Add or clean a bird bath / water source
- Plant early blooming flowers for pollinators
- Leave some brush, cover, or natural shelter areas
- Reduce or avoid unnecessary chemical use
- Add a native plant, shrub, or flowering perennial
- Watch for returning wildlife and note what shows up
- Check nesting spots or birdhouse readiness
- Keep a simple backyard wildlife journal or photo log

### Page 3 — Next steps
- Pick three items to do this week
- Encourage steady improvement over perfection
- Invite them to read the blog for more ideas

## Design Notes
- Keep it short
- Make it printable
- Use clean checkboxes
- Add one or two property photos if helpful
