    The Journey / Publication & keeping in touch

    Advertise With Quiet Backyard Living

    Quiet Backyard Living is a growing editorial-style backyard site focused on practical gardening help, small-space growing, wildlife-friendly ideas, and real backyard projects that feel approachable instead of overcomplicated.

    Ask About Advertising or Partnerships

    Who the site is for

    Quiet Backyard Living is built for people who like practical backyard improvement, useful garden ideas, small projects, herbs, raised beds, bird-friendly spaces, and a warmer more realistic kind of outdoor living.

    • home gardeners and raised-bed growers
    • people improving small or medium backyards
    • bird, pollinator, and backyard wildlife lovers
    • readers who like simple projects and approachable tools

    Best fit partners

    • garden tools and hand tools
    • raised-bed, irrigation, or composting products
    • birding, pollinator, and wildlife-garden brands
    • backyard decor, outdoor lifestyle, and cottage-style brands
    • digital products or useful services that genuinely fit the audience

    Current content lanes

    • practical gardening help
    • companion planting and crop problem solving
    • herb value and small-space growing
    • real backyard projects and wildlife builds

    Simple ways to work together

    • site placements and house-ad-style creative
    • sponsored content that fits the site naturally
    • product features tied to real backyard use
    • affiliate-friendly content where it makes sense

    What matters here

    Anything promoted on Quiet Backyard Living should feel honest, useful, and like a natural fit for the audience. The goal is not clutter. The goal is trust.

    Why this site may be a good fit

    The site is developing a distinctive mix of practical gardening help, editorial-style homepage browsing, and visual backyard project content. That combination makes it a better fit for brands that want a warmer, more personal context than a giant generic gardening database.

    Quiet Backyard Living is still growing, so this page is intentionally simple for now. The best next step is just to start the conversation.
