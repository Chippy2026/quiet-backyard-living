    My First Herb Garden: A Simple 5-Herb Garden I’d Build Again

    If you have never grown herbs before, start here. This simple setup is easy to build, easy to maintain, and gives you herbs you will actually use.

    Quiet Backyard LivingAbout 2 min readRead the story ↓
    Existing QBL illustration

    The five herbs I’d start with

    🌿 Basil

    Fast growing. Useful. Rewarding.

    🌿 Parsley

    Versatile. Forgiving. Useful in dozens of meals.

    🌿 Chives

    One of the easiest herbs you will ever grow.

    🌿 Oregano

    Low maintenance. Great fresh or dried.

    🌿 Thyme

    Compact. Hardy. Useful all season.

    GardeningWhen the basil needs its first haircut ↗The growing guide explains clipping above leaf sets, not just picking random leaves.

    Before you start

    Keep it small. The biggest beginner mistake is trying to build a giant herb garden right away.

    For your first herb garden:

    • 5 containers
    • or one 4x4 raised bed

    Five healthy herbs will teach you more than twenty struggling ones.

    Where to put it:

    • near the kitchen
    • near the patio
    • somewhere you will see every day
    Existing QBL illustration

    Build it in one afternoon

    ①
    Pick a sunny location.
    ②
    Set up containers or a raised bed.
    ③
    Add soil.
    ④
    Plant your herbs.
    ⑤
    Water thoroughly.
    ⑥
    Take a picture. Seriously. You will be amazed how much changes in a few weeks.

    The first month

    Week 1
    Water and watch.
    Week 2
    Learn your herbs. Touch them. Smell them. Learn what they look like.
    Week 3
    Start clipping. Do not be afraid to use them.
    Week 4
    Cook something, even if it is only a few leaves. That first harvest matters.

    Beginner mistakes

    Mistake Better approach
    Too many herbsStart with 5
    Tiny potsUse larger containers
    Hidden locationPut them where you’ll see them
    Forgetting to harvestClip a little every week
    Growing mint with everythingGive mint its own pot
    ⏱️ The 30-second version

    If you only remember five things:

    • start small
    • grow basil, parsley, chives, oregano, and thyme
    • put them near the kitchen
    • harvest often
    • do not overthink it
