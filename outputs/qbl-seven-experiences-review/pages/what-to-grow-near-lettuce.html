    What to grow
    near lettuce.

    Keep it cool.
    Keep it reachable.
    Keep the neighbors gentle.

    Carrots, radishes, scallions, chives and a little dill make a useful starting list. Leave the big summer sprawl out of the lettuce’s way.

    And a little dill?

    A little dill can fit. Keep it from becoming a wall of foliage. Afternoon relief in heat is different from lettuce being smothered by a bigger crop.

    Show me / A reachable patch

    A small salad garden.
    Not a leafy traffic jam.

    Scallions or chives · narrow edge
    Carrots in their own strip
    Early radish pockets
    Lettuce · keep the harvest side reachable

    Arrangement only; not to scale. No plant counts, exact spacing or bed dimensions are implied.

    Cooler timing.

    Lettuce is a cool-season crop. Heat and a dry bed can make the plan harder to manage.

    Steady moisture.

    Avoid the swing between soggy and dried out. Keep checking conditions around the roots.

    Small repeats.

    Try small repeated plantings rather than one large all-at-once patch. Match timing to your conditions.

    Look low / Look closely

    Tender leaves show
    trouble quickly.

    Check folds, leaf undersides and the soil surface. A hole or curled leaf is a clue, not a diagnosis.

    Reference comparison / Slug

    Holes or slime trails?

    Compare what is actually in the bed. Slugs are one possibility; inspect low hiding places and the soil surface.

    Take a look ↗
    Reference comparison / Aphids

    Tiny insects in the folds?

    Look for the insects themselves and sticky residue. Confirm identification before choosing a response.

    Take a look ↗
    Other clues worth checking

    Young plants damaged at soil level can point toward cutworms. Pale winding leaf tunnels can suggest leafminers; earwigs are another possibility discussed in the source. Compare the actual damage and insect rather than treating every chewed leaf alike.

    Don’t crowd it, then forget it.

    Big summer plants, hard drying between waterings and damp hiding spots can all make the bed harder to manage. Review damage early; don’t assume a homemade spray is harmless.
