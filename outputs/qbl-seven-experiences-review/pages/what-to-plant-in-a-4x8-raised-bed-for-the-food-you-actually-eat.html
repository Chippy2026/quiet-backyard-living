    Before anything goes in

    Fewer big plants.
    More useful room.

    01

    Start with size.

    Zucchini can dominate. Cucumbers need support or sprawling room. Mature size matters more than the size of a starter pot.

    02

    Keep access.

    Can you reach the middle, supports and fruit? A productive-looking map is not useful if harvesting becomes a wrestling match.

    03

    Respect the edges.

    Pumpkins, winter squash and sprawling melons usually need more room. An edge experiment is different from a reliable fit.

    The small support crew

    Herbs fit best
    with a purpose.

    Choose a few you’ll cut.

    Try basil near tomatoes or peppers, narrow chives, reachable parsley, and thyme on drier edges. These are starting ideas, not a reason to fill every spare inch.

    Keep the collectors elsewhere.

    Mint belongs in its own pot in this plan. Give a large rosemary or lavender plant room of its own rather than treating it as a tiny permanent tuck-in.
