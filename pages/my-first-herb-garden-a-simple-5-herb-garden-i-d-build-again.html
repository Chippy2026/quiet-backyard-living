My First Herb Garden: A Simple 5-Herb Garden I’d Build Again

If you have never grown herbs before, start here. This simple setup is easy to build, easy to maintain, and gives you herbs you will actually use.

🌱 Keep it small

The biggest beginner mistake is trying to build a giant herb garden right away.

For your first herb garden, a 4x4 raised bed or 5 containers is plenty. Five healthy herbs will teach you more than twenty struggling ones.

What you’ll need

Option A: Containers

  • 5 containers
  • potting mix
  • watering can
  • herb plants

Option B: Raised bed

  • one small raised bed
  • garden soil
  • herb plants
  • watering can

That’s it. No complicated equipment. No expensive systems.

The five herbs I’d start with

🌿 Basil

Fast growing. Useful. Rewarding.

🌿 Parsley

Versatile. Forgiving. Useful in dozens of meals.

🌿 Chives

One of the easiest herbs you will ever grow.

🌿 Oregano

Low maintenance. Great fresh or dried.

🌿 Thyme

Compact. Hardy. Useful all season.

Exactly where to put it

The easy rule: put your herb garden where you will see it every day.

Good locations:

  • near the kitchen
  • near the patio
  • near the back door
  • near a walkway

Avoid:

  • hidden corners
  • places you rarely visit

Build it in one afternoon

  1. Choose your location.
  2. Set up your containers or raised bed.
  3. Fill with soil.
  4. Plant your herbs.
  5. Water thoroughly.
  6. Take a picture. Seriously. You will be amazed how much changes in a few weeks.

The first month

Week 1

Water as needed. Watch for new growth.

Week 2

Get familiar with each herb. Touch them. Smell them. Learn what they look like.

Week 3

Start clipping small amounts. Do not be afraid to use them.

Week 4

Cook with something you grew, even if it is only a few leaves. That first harvest is important.

Beginner mistakes

Mistake Better approach
Too many herbsStart with 5
Tiny potsUse larger containers
Hidden locationPut them where you’ll see them
Forgetting to harvestClip a little every week
Growing mint with everythingGive mint its own pot
⏱️ The 30-second version

If you only remember five things:

  • start small
  • grow basil, parsley, chives, oregano, and thyme
  • put them near the kitchen
  • harvest often
  • do not overthink it
