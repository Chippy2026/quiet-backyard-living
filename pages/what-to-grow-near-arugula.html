What to Grow Near Arugula

Arugula usually does best with the same kind of gentler cooler-season logic that helps lettuce and spinach.

Good nearby friends for arugula

  • Radishes Quick little companion crop for similar timing.
  • Lettuce A natural cool-season neighbor.
  • Spinach Another compatible cool-season leafy crop.
  • Carrots Can work nearby if spacing is handled well.
  • Chives Small and useful without too much crowding.

Did you know?

  • Arugula often bolts faster once heat shows up.
  • Cooler-season neighbors usually make more sense than big summer crops.
  • Timing can matter as much as companionship.

A simple arugula bed example

A simple beginner-friendly mix could look like this:

  • arugula as the main crop
  • radishes tucked nearby
  • lettuce or spinach in the same window
  • chives at the edges

What to be careful about

Do not let larger crops crowd it too fast.

  • Heat can shorten its good window quickly.
  • Keep moisture reasonably steady.

Protect my arugula, what actually helps?

If arugula leaves start getting peppered with holes or chewed hard, the first troublemakers to suspect are usually flea beetles, cabbage worms, aphids, or slugs in damp spells. Arugula is quick, but pests can rough it up fast if you miss the early signs.

  • What to watch for Shot-hole leaves, green caterpillars, sticky growth, slimy chewing, and tender leaves that suddenly look ragged.
  • What naturally helps most Row cover, quick harvest timing, steady moisture, and checking the leaves before damage gets out of hand.
  • Protect the plants early Tender young arugula can get roughed up quickly.
  • Check under the leaves Eggs, aphids, and worms often show up there first.
  • Keep the bed cool and steady Heat plus pest stress makes arugula bolt faster.
  • Harvest rough leaves quickly That keeps the patch cleaner and easier to manage.

Common arugula troublemakers

  • Flea beetles Tiny jumpy beetles that pepper leaves with holes. Quick cure: protect young plants early and keep them growing fast.
  • Cabbage worms Chew tender leaves quickly. Quick cure: hand-pick them early and keep checking.
  • Aphids Build in tender folds and stress the plant. Quick cure: spray them off before colonies deepen.
  • Slugs Can shred tender greens in damp beds. Quick cure: reduce hiding spots and inspect at dusk or early morning.
  • Cabbage loopers Another caterpillar that can quietly chew through leaves. Quick cure: catch them while they are still small.

Best first move

Look at the youngest leaves right away, because arugula tells you fast when flea beetles or caterpillars have moved in.

Good natural arugula pest routine

  • protect young plants early
  • check leaf undersides often
  • spray off aphids before they build
  • watch for slug damage in damp stretches
  • harvest rough leaves quickly

What not to do

  • Do not ignore early shot-hole damage.
  • Do not let arugula stay crowded and stressed in heat.
  • Do not wait too long on caterpillar damage.

Natural remedies people sometimes try

  • Bt (Bacillus thuringiensis) for young cabbage worms and loopers.
  • Insecticidal soap for aphids if sprayed directly.
  • Row cover is one of the best low-tech protections for tender arugula.

A few more arugula basics that really matter

  • Soil pH: arugula often does well around 6.0 to 7.0
  • Cool weather: usually helps a lot
  • Steady moisture: keeps growth more tender
  • Quick succession planting: often works well
  • Sun: full sun to light shade can work

Bottom line

If you like arugula, keep the bed practical, give the crop what it actually needs, and add just a couple helpful neighbors instead of chasing a perfect chart.
