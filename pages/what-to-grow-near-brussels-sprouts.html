What to Grow Near Brussels Sprouts

Brussels sprouts need room, time, and usually a little patience, so nearby crops should help the bed stay balanced instead of crowded.

Good nearby friends for brussels sprouts

  • Dill A classic brassica-side herb.
  • Onions Strong-smelling and easy to fit nearby.
  • Garlic Another useful allium option.
  • Nasturtiums Helpful flower ally.
  • Marigolds Easy mixed-bed diversity.

Did you know?

  • Brussels sprouts are not a quick crop.
  • They often take longer and get taller than people expect.
  • Good spacing pays off with them.

A simple brussels sprouts bed example

A simple beginner-friendly mix could look like this:

  • brussels sprouts as the main crop
  • dill nearby
  • onions or garlic at edges
  • nasturtiums nearby

What to be careful about

Do not crowd them early and regret it later.

  • Watch airflow as the plants get taller and fuller.
  • Be patient with the timeline.

A few more brussels sprouts basics that really matter

  • Soil pH: brussels sprouts often do well around 6.5 to 7.5
  • Cool weather: usually suits them best
  • Rich soil: helps support the long growing season
  • Moisture: steady moisture matters
  • Spacing: important because they can get bigger than expected

Bottom line

If you like brussels sprouts, keep the bed practical, give the crop what it actually needs, and add just a couple helpful neighbors instead of chasing a perfect chart.
