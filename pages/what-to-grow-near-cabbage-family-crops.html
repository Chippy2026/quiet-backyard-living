What to Grow Near Cabbage Family Crops

Cabbage family crops often benefit from a few strong helpers nearby, especially when pests are part of the problem.

Cabbage Family Crops companions at a glance

Best easy companions

Basil, chives, onions, parsley, and marigolds are some of the easiest useful nearby support plants if they fit the crop and the season.

What helps most

Airflow, spacing, support, and a few smaller helpers usually matter more than trying to build a perfect companion-planting chart.

Good use of the edges

Use the edges for herbs, onions, chives, or flowers, not for another big thirsty main crop.

What to avoid

Do not let the bed turn into a crowded plant tangle where airflow disappears and every support plant becomes a competitor.

Best cabbage family crops support crew

  • Basil for easy herb value nearby
  • Marigolds for flowers and edge life
  • Onions or chives for compact lower growth
  • Parsley for another useful edible
  • Lettuce only early, before cabbage family crops take over

Why these cabbage family crops pairings actually make sense

  • Basil works because it stays useful without taking over. It fits the same general growing rhythm as the crop and gives you an herb you will probably reach for often.
  • Marigolds work best as edge flowers, not as magic pest shields. They help the bed feel alive, bring in some beneficial activity, and visually break things up.
  • Onions and chives fit because they stay lower and simpler. They can use little pockets of space without becoming a huge shading problem.
  • Parsley earns its place by being practical. It gives you another real edible without demanding the same same big main-crop footprint.
  • Lettuce only works early. It can be a nice temporary front-edge crop, but later in the season the main crop usually outgrows it and starts winning the light-and-moisture battle.

What usually goes wrong

  • too many medium and large plants packed together
  • not enough airflow around the main crop
  • lettuce or other smaller crops getting buried in shade later
  • thinking “companion planting” means every inch needs a plant

A simple cabbage family crops bed layout idea

A beginner-friendly cabbage family crops bed often works best when the cabbage family crops stay the obvious main crop and the helpers stay in supporting roles.

  • cabbage family crops spaced as the main center or back crop
  • basil tucked near a few plants, not jammed everywhere
  • marigolds on edges or corners
  • chives or onions in lower side pockets
  • lettuce only as an early front-edge crop before the cabbage family crops get dominant

What to be careful about

Cabbage Family Crops need more room, airflow, sun, and moisture consistency than people sometimes expect. So even a “good companion” can become a problem if the bed gets too crowded.

  • Do not jam too many thirsty plants together.
  • Give cabbage family crops room to breathe.
  • Let shorter support plants stay support plants, not full competitors.
  • Be careful with lettuce later in the season once cabbage family crops get large and dominant.

Protect my cabbage family crops, what actually helps?

If your brassicas are getting full of holes fast, the first pests to suspect are usually cabbage worms, cabbage loopers, aphids, or flea beetles. These crops often attract attention quickly, so early checking matters a lot.

  • What to watch for Chewed leaves, green caterpillars, clusters of gray-green aphids, tiny shot holes, and white butterflies hovering around the bed.
  • What naturally helps most Row cover early, hand-picking caterpillars, crushing egg clusters, and checking leaf undersides often.
  • Use row cover early Brassicas are great candidates for row cover before pest pressure gets heavy.
  • Keep the bed easier to inspect Good spacing and less clutter make it easier to spot worms and eggs early.
  • Stay steady on plant health Consistent water and decent soil help brassicas handle pressure better.
  • Clean up rough leaves Remove badly infested foliage instead of leaving a pest nursery in the bed.

Common brassica troublemakers

  • Cabbage worms Green caterpillars that chew leaves fast. Quick cure: hand-pick them and check leaf undersides for eggs often.
  • Cabbage loopers Similar leaf-chewing caterpillars that move with a looping motion. Quick cure: inspect regularly and remove them early.
  • Aphids Sap-suckers that can cluster deep in the leaves. Quick cure: spray them off with water before the colony gets thick.
  • Flea beetles Tiny beetles that pepper leaves with little holes. Quick cure: protect young plants early and keep them growing fast.
  • Root maggots Underground pests that can weaken or collapse young plants. Quick cure: use row cover early and rotate where you plant brassicas.

Best first move

Check the undersides of leaves first. That is where eggs, aphids, and small worms often show up before the damage gets obvious.

Good natural brassica pest routine

  • use row cover early if needed
  • check leaves every day or two
  • hand-pick worms fast
  • remove rough infested leaves
  • do not ignore the first small holes

What not to do

  • Do not wait until the leaves are skeletonized.
  • Do not let white cabbage butterflies fool you into thinking nothing is happening.
  • Do not keep planting brassicas in the same tired trouble spot.

Natural remedies people sometimes try

  • Bt (Bacillus thuringiensis) for young cabbage worms and loopers.
  • Neem oil for some early soft-bodied pests, used carefully and not as a cure-all.
  • Row cover is still one of the most useful low-tech protections people rely on here.

A few more cabbage family crops basics that really matter

  • Soil pH: many brassicas like roughly 6.5 to 7.5
  • Rich soil: they usually appreciate good fertility
  • Moisture: steady water helps avoid stress
  • Cooler weather: many do best outside peak heat
  • Spacing: important for airflow and head formation
  • Observation: catch pest pressure early

Bottom line

If you like cabbage family crops, keep the bed simple, give the plants what they really need, and add just a couple helpful neighbors instead of trying to force a perfect companion chart.
