What to Grow Near Cabbage Family Crops

Cabbage family crops often benefit from a few strong helpers nearby, especially when pests are part of the problem.

Good nearby friends for cabbage family crops

  • Dill Classic brassica-side helper.
  • Thyme A compact herb option with old-school companion appeal.
  • Onions Simple strong-smelling neighbor.
  • Garlic Useful edge plant and traditional companion.
  • Nasturtiums Helpful flower ally.
  • Marigolds Easy diversity and color.

Did you know?

  • Brassicas often attract attention from pests quickly.
  • Mixed beds can help feel more balanced than one big brassica block.
  • Old-school gardeners kept returning to herbs and flowers for a reason.

A simple cabbage family crops bed example

A simple beginner-friendly mix could look like this:

  • cabbage family crop as the main focus
  • dill nearby
  • onions or garlic around edges
  • nasturtiums or marigolds nearby

What to be careful about

Watch for crowding and airflow issues.

  • Inspect often for pest pressure.
  • Do not overplant every “good companion” at once.
  • Give these crops enough room to mature well.

Natural Pest Deterrents for Cabbage Family Crops

Cabbage Family Crops usually does better when the bed is set up to stay healthier and less inviting to trouble in the first place. Natural pest deterrents make the most sense here as part of a better system, not as a last-minute panic fix.

  • More bed diversity can help the space feel less like one plain easy target.
  • Good spacing and airflow help reduce the cramped stagnant conditions that make trouble worse.
  • Steady moisture helps keep plants less stressed and more resilient.
  • Quick cleanup and early checking still go a long way in a backyard bed.
  • Simple natural support usually works better than throwing random fixes at a problem too late.

What helps most

  • healthier plants
  • less crowding
  • better airflow
  • early attention
  • a calmer more balanced bed

A few more cabbage family crops basics that really matter

  • Soil pH: many brassicas like roughly 6.5 to 7.5
  • Rich soil: they usually appreciate good fertility
  • Moisture: steady water helps avoid stress
  • Cooler weather: many do best outside peak heat
  • Spacing: important for airflow and head formation
  • Observation: catch pest pressure early

Bottom line

If you like cabbage family crops, keep the bed simple, give the plants what they really need, and add just a couple helpful neighbors instead of trying to force a perfect companion chart.
