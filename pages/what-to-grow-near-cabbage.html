What to Grow Near Cabbage

Cabbage usually likes a few steady helpers nearby, especially if you want the bed to feel more balanced than one big target for pests.

Good nearby friends for cabbage

  • Dill A classic companion for cabbage-family crops.
  • Thyme Compact herb option.
  • Onions Strong-smelling and easy to mix in.
  • Garlic Another useful edge crop.
  • Nasturtiums Helpful flower support in a mixed bed.

Did you know?

  • Cabbage often benefits from the same mixed-bed logic as other brassicas.
  • Spacing and airflow matter more than people think.
  • A little diversity can make the bed feel more resilient.

A simple cabbage bed example

A simple beginner-friendly mix could look like this:

  • cabbage as the main crop
  • dill nearby
  • garlic or onions around edges
  • nasturtiums nearby

What to be careful about

Do not cram cabbage into an overcrowded patch.

  • Watch for airflow issues and pest buildup.
  • Give heads enough room to form well.

Natural Pest Deterrents for Cabbage

Cabbage usually does better when the bed is set up to stay healthier and less inviting to trouble in the first place. Natural pest deterrents make the most sense here as part of a better system, not as a last-minute panic fix.

  • More bed diversity can help the space feel less like one plain easy target.
  • Good spacing and airflow help reduce the cramped stagnant conditions that make trouble worse.
  • Steady moisture helps keep plants less stressed and more resilient.
  • Quick cleanup and early checking still go a long way in a backyard bed.
  • Simple natural support usually works better than throwing random fixes at a problem too late.

What helps most

  • healthier plants
  • less crowding
  • better airflow
  • early attention
  • a calmer more balanced bed

A few more cabbage basics that really matter

  • Soil pH: cabbage often does well around 6.5 to 7.5
  • Rich soil: supports head growth
  • Cool weather: usually helps more than heat
  • Moisture: steady watering matters
  • Spacing: important for healthier heads

Bottom line

If you like cabbage, keep the bed practical, give the crop what it actually needs, and add just a couple helpful neighbors instead of chasing a perfect chart.
