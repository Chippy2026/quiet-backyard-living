What to Grow Near Strawberries

Strawberries usually do best with neighbors that stay fairly gentle, useful, and not too competitive around their shallow root zone.

Good nearby friends for strawberries

  • Thyme Low-growing and useful without overwhelming the bed.
  • Lettuce Can fit nicely in some cooler-season timing.
  • Spinach Another gentle leafy option in the right timing.
  • Onions A simple edge-friendly crop.
  • Chives Small and easy to mix in nearby.

Did you know?

  • Strawberries are often thirstier and more delicate than they first appear.
  • Low-growing companions usually make more sense here than bulky ones.
  • A tidy-looking bed can still be very productive.

A simple strawberries bed example

A simple beginner-friendly mix could look like this:

  • strawberries as the main crop
  • thyme nearby
  • chives or onions at edges
  • lettuce or spinach in cooler timing

What to be careful about

Do not crowd the crowns too much.

  • Watch moisture in warm weather.
  • Be careful with aggressive neighboring roots.
  • Keep fruit off soggy soil when possible.

A few more strawberries basics that really matter

  • Soil pH: strawberries often prefer slightly acidic soil around 5.5 to 6.8
  • Sun: full sun is usually best
  • Moisture: steady moisture matters
  • Mulch: helps keep fruit cleaner and moisture steadier
  • Airflow: helps reduce trouble around fruit
  • Renewal: beds often need refreshing over time

Bottom line

If you like strawberries, keep the bed simple, give the plants what they really need, and add just a couple helpful neighbors instead of trying to force a perfect companion chart.
