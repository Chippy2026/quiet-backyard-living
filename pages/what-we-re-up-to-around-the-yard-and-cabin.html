The story so far

It started rough, unfinished, and more hopeful than polished

We did not begin with a dream property that was ready to photograph. We started with 1.7 acres, power, a bad very brackish well, a primitive half-started cabin shell, a garage, and a strange little shack built right next to a giant pine tree like nobody had fully thought it through.

A lot of it was rough. A lot of it was half started. Some of it felt promising, some of it felt questionable, and some of it only made sense because we could already see the version of the place it might become if we kept showing up.

Early chapters

Before the comfort, there was the make-do stage

Some of the strongest early parts of this story came before anything looked finished, air mattresses, unfinished walls, the wood stove, improvised kitchen setups, and the kind of cold-weather cabin days where just staying warm together felt like enough for the moment.

Those photos matter because they show what the place really was before the better-looking versions ever arrived. The beginning was not pretty, but it was real.

What this book holds

The good weeks, the frustrating weeks, and the weird little in-between ones

This running page is meant to hold all of it, the project misses, the paint that looked wrong in real life, the grapes the wildlife beat us to, the practical improvements that made the cabin work better, the family builds, the garden setbacks, the comeback attempts, and the little moments that gave the place heart.

So this is not a one-time article. It is the unfinished book version of Quiet Backyard Living, and we will keep adding chapters as the story keeps moving.
