Quiet Backyard Living Ongoing Story

What We’re Up To Around the Yard and Cabin

This is the running story side of Quiet Backyard Living. Not just finished reveals, the real week-by-week and season-by-season thread of what we are building, fixing, trying, messing up, learning from, and slowly turning into something better.

Start here

We did not begin with a polished setup. We started with 1.7 acres, a primitive half-started cabin shell, a garage, a strange little shack in the weirdest place, power, and a bad very brackish well.

Since then, Quiet Backyard Living has slowly become part backyard project journal, part garden experiment, part recovery story, and part record of what happens when you keep showing up to a place that still needs work.

This page is where that bigger story keeps moving forward.

Come back and check in

If you like following real progress instead of polished pretending, this is the page to check every week or two. We will keep adding to the story as the yard, garden, and cabin keep changing.

Where it began

The rough beginning is part of the point

Some of the strongest parts of this story are the early ones, rough interiors, unfinished spaces, air mattresses, wood stove warmth, overgrown areas, and the kind of property decisions that only make sense because you were trying to make do with what you had.

That is why the beginning matters here. The place did not arrive finished, and neither did we.

What this includes

The good weeks, the frustrating weeks, and the weird little in-between ones

This running page is meant to hold all of it, garden wins, project misses, critters beating us to the grapes, paint colors that looked wrong in real life, slow cabin improvements, practical infrastructure work, and the little things that made a place feel more peaceful even when life itself was messy.

What to expect next

We are still building the story as we go

Next rounds will include more cabin progress, garden updates, what worked and what failed, and the real-life before-and-after threads that are starting to take shape. This page is meant to read more like chapters than a one-time article.
