## Board 6
### Board name
Backyard Checklists & Guides

### Description
Helpful backyard checklists, planning pages, and simple guides for creating a more peaceful, wildlife-friendly outdoor space.

### Step by step
1. In Pinterest, click **Create** or the **+** button
2. Click **Board**
3. For the board name, paste:
   - `Backyard Checklists & Guides`
4. Create the board
5. Open the board
6. Click **Edit board** or the three dots
7. Paste this description:
   - `Helpful backyard checklists, planning pages, and simple guides for creating a more peaceful, wildlife-friendly outdoor space.`
8. Save
