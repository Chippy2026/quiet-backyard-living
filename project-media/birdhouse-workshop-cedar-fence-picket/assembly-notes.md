# Cedar Fence Picket Birdhouse — Assembly Notes

These notes were extracted from Jeff's assembly instruction photos and should be paired with the corresponding images in `details/`.

## Build flow

### 1. Layout stick pieces on the dogear base
- Layout stick pieces on dogear base
- Glue or pin nail in place

### 2. Build the log cabin rows
- Keep assembling sticks in a log cabin fashion
- One row at a time
- Make sure to overlap every other row
- Glue or pin nail each row

### 3. Total row count
- There should be a total of 7 rows

### 4. Gable filler pieces
- Glue or pin nail 2 sets of 2 small sticks together
- These become the 2 gable end filler pieces

### 5. Front, back, and filler gable pieces
- Next, glue and pin nail the front, back, and filler gable pieces

### 6. Roof assembly
- Glue and pin nail the 2 roof pieces together

### 7. Final roof placement
- Finally, place the roof
- Do not glue the roof on
- You need a way to clean the birdhouse out
- Can run a rope with eyelets if desired
