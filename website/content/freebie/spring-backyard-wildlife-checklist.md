# Spring Backyard Wildlife Checklist

**Simple steps to help your yard feel more alive this season**

---

## Welcome
Spring is one of the best times to make small changes that attract more birds, butterflies, pollinators, and peaceful backyard wildlife.

You do not need a perfect yard or a huge budget. A few thoughtful improvements can make your outdoor space feel more alive, more beautiful, and more enjoyable.

Use this checklist as a simple way to get started.

---

## Your Spring Checklist

### Water
- [ ] Clean and refill bird baths or shallow water sources
- [ ] Make sure wildlife can safely reach the water
- [ ] Refresh water often, especially as temperatures rise

### Feeders
- [ ] Clean bird feeders before heavy spring use
- [ ] Refill feeders with fresh seed
- [ ] Set out hummingbird feeders when the season starts in your area
- [ ] Place feeders where birds feel safe and can reach cover nearby

### Plants and Flowers
- [ ] Add early blooming flowers for bees and butterflies
- [ ] Plant at least one native flower, shrub, or perennial
- [ ] Look for ways to add color, nectar, berries, or seed sources over time

### Shelter and Habitat
- [ ] Leave some natural cover such as shrubs, brush, or tucked-away corners
- [ ] Check birdhouses or nesting spots for seasonal readiness
- [ ] Avoid over-cleaning every natural area in the yard

### Safer Yard Habits
- [ ] Reduce or avoid unnecessary chemical use
- [ ] Leave some areas a little wilder instead of trimming everything back
- [ ] Watch for nesting activity before doing major cleanup

### Observe and Enjoy
- [ ] Notice which birds, pollinators, or small wildlife already visit your yard
- [ ] Take a photo of one wildlife moment this week
- [ ] Keep a simple notebook or photo log of what shows up
