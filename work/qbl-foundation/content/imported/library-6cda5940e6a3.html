Working with Quiet Backyard Living / A proposed approach

Useful things.
Honestly lived with.

A good partnership should belong in the life we’re building, and give the reader something worth keeping.

Who this publication is for

People making
a place of their own.

QBL speaks to people who want to spend more time outside, grow a little food, make useful things, and build a good everyday life toward the years ahead. They may start with a patio pot, a small yard, or a rough cabin. The shared interest is making a place that fits real life.

Jeff and Vanessa bring the reader along while they learn. The work, the mistakes, the changing plans, and the outcomes are part of what makes the story worth trusting.

Where a collaboration could belong

Inside something
we would be doing anyway.

01 / A real project

Materials with a purpose.

A tool, garden material, or piece of outdoor equipment that helps with a project the place actually needs. The useful part is the story and what the reader learns.

02 / A return visit

What happened later.

Come back after the build, the planting, or a stretch of use. There should be room for what worked, what did not, and what we would change.

03 / A season together

Follow the whole experiment.

A growing season, a practical cabin improvement, or a habit that makes life outside more enjoyable. A patient story can say more than a one-day reveal.

An approach we could stand behind

Keep the reader
in the room.

A partnership should be clearly identified. The outcome should remain honest. The product should fit the work, the place, and the scale of ordinary people doing useful things with what they have.
